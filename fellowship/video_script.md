# Video Talking Points (max 5 minutes)

The apply page shows the two exact questions — check them first and adapt. The two
most likely shapes are covered below. Budget ~2 minutes per answer plus a 30-second
intro. Speak it, don't read it — bullet points, not a teleprompter script.

## Opening (30s)

- Angela Reinhold, independent AI safety researcher, founder of Lorien's Library.
- One-sentence thesis: "The moment an LLM system remembers anything across sessions,
  it becomes safety-critical — and I build and test the discipline for how it
  remembers."

## If asked: why AI safety / why this fellowship? (~2 min)

- Came to alignment through building, not through a lab: sustained longitudinal
  interaction with LLM systems exposed a failure class most evaluations never see —
  what happens *across* sessions, not within one.
- Persistent memory is shipping in production products right now; false memories,
  epistemic contamination, and correction failure are arriving faster than the
  evaluation tooling for them.
- I've taken this as far as an independent researcher can alone: 11 preprints, a
  working open-source system with 380 tests, a published dataset, an operational
  deployment. What I don't have is mentorship, peers, and the structure to turn N=1
  evidence into controlled, generalizable results. That's exactly what this
  fellowship is for.

## If asked: what would you research? (~2 min)

- One question: does provenance-aware write discipline measurably reduce
  false-memory persistence in stateful LLM systems?
- Three controlled evaluations — seeded false memories, correction retention,
  adversarial insertion — comparing disciplined memory vs. unrestricted memory vs.
  stateless, using my existing system as its own ablation control.
- Deliverable in 8 weeks: a written report *and* an open-source false-memory
  persistence benchmark other memory systems can be scored against.
- Feasible in 8 weeks precisely because the infrastructure already exists and is
  public — the fellowship funds the science, not the plumbing.

## Close (15s)

- "Everything I've claimed is verifiable — my evidence matrix links every claim to
  its proof and its limitation. I'd love the chance to do this properly, with a
  cohort."

## Recording notes

- Under 5 minutes hard limit — rehearse once with a timer; aim for 4:15.
- One take you're happy with beats ten perfect fragments. Plain background, decent
  light, phone at eye level is fine.
- They're selecting for rigorous thinking, not production value.
